# Content Quality Audit Reference

Use this as a compact checklist rather than a rigid score engine.

## Core Checks

### Intent and Usefulness

- Does the piece answer the user's likely question early?
- Is the page type aligned with the query?
- Is the scope complete enough for the claim it makes?

### Evidence and Specificity

- Are there concrete examples, data, or demonstrations?
- Are claims supported or merely asserted?
- Is anything obviously stale or vague?

### Experience and Expertise

- Is there first-hand experience, testing, or direct observation where relevant?
- Is the author or organization identifiable?
- Are credentials or context appropriate to the topic?

### Trust and Disclosure

- Are sponsorships, affiliations, or incentives disclosed when relevant?
- Are comparisons fair and non-misleading?
- Is contact or accountability information present where appropriate?

### Readability and Structure

- Is the page easy to scan?
- Are sections logically organized?
- Are examples, tables, FAQs, or summaries used where helpful?

### Maintenance

- Are dates or facts current enough?
- Does the content contradict itself?
- Does it look maintained rather than abandoned?

## Simple Verdict Model

- `BLOCK`: not safe or not credible enough to publish
- `NEEDS WORK`: potentially publishable after fixes
- `READY WITH MINOR FIXES`: sound, but still has polish items
